F2 · Task and Data Plan: Community Volunteer Application

This inventory documents all 10 controls included in the volunteer application flow to satisfy data minimization and WCAG requirements.

Visible label / question Why necessary now? Required? Native control/type Submitted name/value Autocomplete/inputmode Constraint/instruction Sensitivity and minimization decision
Full Name Required to properly address and log the volunteer applicant in our directory. Yes input / text name="fullname" autocomplete="name" required; text format Low sensitivity; restricted strictly to name for identification.
Email Address Needed to send application confirmation and scheduling details. Yes input / email name="email" autocomplete="email" required; valid email format Moderate sensitivity; used solely for volunteer coordination communication.
Phone Number Required for urgent notifications or day-of shift schedule reminders. Yes input / tel name="phone" autocomplete="tel" required; numeric phone pattern Moderate sensitivity; protected communication channel.
Primary Area of Interest Allows matching volunteer skills and preferences to active community programs. Yes select / dropdown name="interest" N/A required; selection required Low sensitivity; preference data only.
Weekly Availability Ensures applicants are scheduled only during times they are free. Yes input / checkbox (group) name="availability" value="weekday/weekend" N/A At least one option required Low sensitivity; scheduling availability only.
Preferred Session Time Determines whether morning or afternoon shift rosters fit the applicant. Yes input / radio (group) name="session" value="morning / afternoon" N/A required; mutually exclusive choice Low sensitivity; preference selection.
Hours Available per Week Enables program leads to cap or allocate weekly volunteer shift hours. Yes input / number name="weeklyhours" N/A required; min="1", max="40" Low sensitivity; numerical tracking metric.
Emergency Contact Name Necessary for on-site health or safety emergencies during volunteer shifts. Yes input / text name="emergencycontact" autocomplete="off" required; text format Moderate sensitivity; collected strictly for safety protocol.
Relevant Skills or Experience Helps coordinators place experienced volunteers in specialized support roles. No textarea name="skills" N/A Optional; max 300 characters Low sensitivity; optional background detail.
Agreement & Privacy Consent Confirms applicant agreement to organization code of conduct and privacy policy. Yes input / checkbox name="consent" value="agreed" N/A required; must be checked Legal compliance requirement.
